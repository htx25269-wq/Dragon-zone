TAWHID

WHAT IS TAWHID?

The meaning of Tawheed is: To single out Allah Ta'ala with all His rights, to attribute Allah Ta'ala's qualities exclusively to Him, and not to any other.


Types of Tawheed:

  • 1. Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah
  • 2. Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah
  • 3. Tawheed al-Asma was-Sifat
