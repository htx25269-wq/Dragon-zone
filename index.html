TAWHID (তাওহীদ)

What is Tawhid?

Tawhid means to single out Allah alone in His Lordship (Rububiyyah), Worship (Uluhiyyah), and Names & Attributes (Asma wa Sifat).

Types of Tawhid:

  1. Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah: Affirming that Allah alone is the Creator, Sustainer, and Disposer of all affairs.
  2. Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah: Directing all acts of worship (Salah, Du'a, Sacrifice, etc.) to Allah alone.
  3. Tawheed al-Asma wa Sifat: Affirming the Names and Attributes of Allah as mentioned in the Quran and Sunnah without distortion or negation.

The opposite of Tawhid is Shirk, which is the greatest sin.

AQIDAH & MANHAZ

Aqidah is the firm belief in the heart regarding Allah, His Angels, Books, Messengers, the Last Day, and Qadr (Divine Decree).

We follow the Aqidah of the Salaf-us-Salih (Righteous Predecessors) — the Sahaba, Tabi'in, and those who followed them in righteousness.

Important Principles:

  • Returning to the Quran and Authentic Sunnah
  • Avoiding innovation (Bid'ah) in religion
  • Loving and hating for the sake of Allah
  • Staying away from Ahlul Bid'ah (People of Innovation)

JIHAD FEE-SABILILLAH

Jihad means to strive and struggle in the way of Allah. It includes:

  • Striving against one's own soul (Jihad an-Nafs)
  • Calling to Tawhid and warning against Shirk
  • Physical Jihad (Qital) when necessary under legitimate Islamic leadership

What the Scholars Said:

The Four Imams (Abu Hanifa, Malik, Shafi'i, Ahmad) and the Salaf unanimously agreed that Jihad remains until the Day of Judgment.

Allah says: "And fight in the way of Allah those who fight you but do not transgress..." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:190)
